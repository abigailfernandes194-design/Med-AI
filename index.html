Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common doubts. Still stuck? Ask the chat assistant.

Is the AI Health Check a real diagnosis?

No. It compares your symptoms with a small demonstration database for learning purposes. Always consult a qualified doctor for medical concerns.

How do I book an appointment?

Open the Appointment section, enter your details, choose a department and date, then confirm.

Is my personal information safe?

Privacy is a core challenge in healthcare AI. In this demo, form details stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 112 or ambulance service 108 straight away. Do not wait for an online result.

Which departments are available?

General Medicine, Cardiology, Pediatrics, Dermatology and Orthopedics.

Benefits and challenges

Benefits

Challenges

  • High implementation cost
  • Need for trained professionals
  • Technology limitations
  • Lack of trust or acceptance
