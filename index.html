Benefits and challenges

AI in healthcare brings real promise alongside real responsibilities.

Benefits

  • Early identification of possible health concerns
  • Faster analysis of information
  • Improved patient monitoring
  • Reduced administrative workload
  • Better access to health information

Challenges

  • Patient data privacy concerns
  • High implementation cost
  • Need for trained professionals
  • Technology limitations
  • Lack of trust or acceptance
